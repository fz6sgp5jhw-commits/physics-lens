Naming the forces

A force is a push or pull from something you can point to. Go body by body and ask what touches it. Then add gravity.

The block has two forces and only one is drawn. What acts on it the whole time, even at rest?

Take care: the tension is not the weight while the block speeds up.

That is both forces

Weight down, tension up. Their difference is what speeds the block up, so its equation writes itself from your drawing:

m g − T = m a

The same T also turns the disc. That shared pull is what binds the two bodies, so the next line is for the disc.
